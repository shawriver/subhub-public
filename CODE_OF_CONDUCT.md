# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## 中文

请保持尊重、具体和建设性的讨论。欢迎不同经验、语言和背景的贡献者。不得骚扰、歧视、泄露他人隐私、发布凭据或进行人身攻击。维护者可以移除违反本准则的内容，并限制重复违规者参与。

## English

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Contributors of all experience levels, languages, and backgrounds are welcome. Harassment, discrimination, privacy disclosure, credential publication, and personal attacks are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove violating content and restrict repeated violations.
